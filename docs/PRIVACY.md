# MileWorth — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 9 June 2026

MileWorth ("we", "our", or "the app") helps you automatically track your driving
trips and calculate potential mileage tax deductions. This policy explains what
we collect, why, and your choices. We try to keep it plain-English.

> **Action required before publishing:** replace `support@mileworth.app` below
> with a real contact email you monitor, and host this page at a public URL
> (Google Play requires a privacy-policy link). See `docs/STORE_LISTING.md` for
> hosting options.

## Information we collect

- **Account information** — the email address and password you use to create an
  account. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash; we never store or see
  your plain-text password.
- **Location data** — your device's GPS location, including **in the background**
  while a drive is being recorded. We use this solely to measure trip distance
  and to detect the start and end of a drive. Routes are recorded as the
  start/end coordinates of each trip.
- **Trip and expense data** — distance, time, business/personal classification,
  computed deduction values, and any expenses or receipt photos you choose to
  add.
- **Purchase information** — when you subscribe, Google Play (or the App Store)
  processes the payment and gives us a purchase token we use to confirm your
  subscription status. We never receive your card or payment details.

We do **not** sell your data, and we do **not** use third-party advertising or
tracking SDKs.

## How we use your information

- To record drives and calculate mileage deductions.
- To sync your data across sessions and devices via our backend.
- To manage your account and subscription.
- To operate, secure, and improve the app.

## Where your data is stored

Your data is sent over HTTPS to our backend service (hosted on Render) and
stored in a MongoDB Atlas database. Access is restricted by authentication.

## Background location disclosure

MileWorth requests **"Allow all the time"** location access so it can record a
drive even when the app is not open. Location is collected only while a trip is
being tracked, for the purpose of mileage logging. You can disable automatic
tracking or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings; the
app remains usable with manual trip entry.

## Your choices and rights

- **Access / export** — you can export your trips as PDF or CSV from the Reports
  screen.
- **Deletion** — to delete your account and associated data, contact us at the
  email below and we will remove it.
- **Permissions** — you can revoke location, camera, or photo access in your
  device settings at any time.

## Children

MileWorth is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in
your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; we will revise the effective date above and, for
material changes, notify you in the app.
