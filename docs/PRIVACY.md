# MileWorth — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 13 August 2026

MileWorth ("we", "our", or "the app") helps you automatically track your driving
trips and calculate potential mileage tax deductions. This policy explains what
we collect, why, and your choices. We try to keep it plain-English.

## The short version

**MileWorth stores everything on your device and sends none of it to us.** There
is no account to create, no sign-in, and no server that receives your trips,
expenses, or location. We cannot see your data.

## Information we collect

We — the developers of MileWorth — collect **nothing**. The app does not
transmit your data to us, and we operate no service that receives it.

The app stores the following **locally on your device**:

- **Location data** — your device's GPS location, including **in the background**
  while a drive is being recorded. It is used solely to measure trip distance and
  to detect the start and end of a drive, and it stays on the device.
- **Trip and expense data** — distance, time, business/personal classification,
  computed deduction values, and any expenses or receipt photos you choose to
  add.
- **Preferences** — your mileage rate, currency, and tracking settings.

We do **not** sell your data, we do **not** use third-party advertising or
analytics SDKs, and there are no accounts, passwords, or in-app purchases.

## How your information is used

- To record drives and calculate mileage deductions on your device.
- To produce the summaries, insights, and reports shown in the app.

All of this is computed locally. Nothing is uploaded.

## Where your data is stored

In the app's private storage on your device, which is sandboxed by the operating
system so other apps cannot read it. Your data is included in a device backup
only if you have enabled one with Apple or Google; that backup is governed by
their privacy policies, not ours.

## Third-party services

Two features reach the network. Both are on-demand, and neither sends us
anything — we still receive no data at all.

- **Google Maps** — the trip detail screen draws your recorded route on a map.
  Rendering that map requires the Google Maps SDK to contact Google's servers for
  map tiles, which necessarily reveals the map area being viewed. Governed by
  [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Your trip list,
  expenses, and receipts are never sent to Google.
- **Navigation ("Navigate" button)** — when *you* tap Navigate, MileWorth hands
  that single destination's coordinates to your phone's maps app (Google Maps on
  Android, Apple Maps on iOS) so it can give you turn-by-turn directions. That
  coordinate is shared with the maps app only at the moment you tap, it is
  governed by that app's own privacy policy, and MileWorth sends nothing else —
  not your trip history, not your other locations. If you never tap Navigate,
  nothing is ever shared this way.

## Background location disclosure

MileWorth requests **"Allow all the time"** location access so it can record a
drive even when the app is not open. Location is collected only while a trip is
being tracked, for the purpose of mileage logging, and is written only to
on-device storage. You can disable automatic tracking or revoke location
permission at any time in your device settings; the app remains usable with
manual trip entry.

## Your choices and rights

- **Access / export** — you can export your trips as a CSV file from the Reports
  screen and share it wherever you choose. The file is generated on your device;
  it goes only where you send it.
- **Deletion** — because your data never leaves your device, you delete it by
  clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. There is no server-side copy,
  so there is nothing to request from us.
- **Permissions** — you can revoke location, camera, or photo access in your
  device settings at any time.

## Children

MileWorth is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in
your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; we will revise the effective date above and, for
material changes, notify you in the app.
